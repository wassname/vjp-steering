## 3. One paper-grounded possibility considered, not selected

Primary source: cached [paper excerpts](../20260907_j_lens_dictionary_control/science-update/paper-evidence.md), original https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html. Cached paper SHA256 **2c1162efd2e37ebad2c73a39120e0401a827162c66fc7cfa56e6e98fd1c5c502** is the excerpt's recorded provenance, not a fresh web fetch. Unresolved figure references remain unresolved. These are paper mechanisms on different models/tasks, not established Qwen benchmark results.

### Mechanism and exact candidate boundary

At paper line283:

> “For each two-hop prompt, we fit a probe for the unspoken intermediate: the mean residual-stream activation over a set of prompts that imply the same intermediate through different surface cues and ask different questions about it, minus the mean over all intermediates.”

The same paragraph specifies a **nonnegative GP k=25 decomposition**, distinct from the concept-reporting GP16 construction at line213. At line259:

> “For each prompt, we first confirm that the intermediate concept appears in the J-lens at intermediate model layers”.

At line287 the remainder's effect is tested with the identified intermediate's J-lens coordinates clamped to clean values; its reported success falls from28% to6%. The paper does not infer a useful task representation merely from a layer-state difference. Likewise line207 says the representation is verbalizable **“under appropriate conditions, rather than unconditional impulses to verbalize a particular output.”**

**Single candidate considered:** replace the topic/persona source with a probe of the model's *inferred existence/validity assessment* of a named methodology, estimated across independently labeled, lexically varied real/fabricated cases and different downstream questions. Subtract the common intermediate mean, decompose with the paper's GP25 rule, and only then consider exchanging its identified assessment components to alter fabrication handling. This is an adaptation of paper line283, **not** a claim that increasing16 to25 fixes anything, nor a new synonym list for the failed fixed-token experiment.

It is genuinely distinct in intended source semantics from “Tell me about sycophancy” and explicit “Answer as someone who…” sources. It is **not yet an executable repair**: the two task states, cue-diverse source inventory, valid model-inferred labels, consuming operation and causal support are missing. Treating the frozen words true/false as the proven intermediate, selecting tokens from DEV errors, or calling the old persona source an implicit task probe would erase exactly the distinction the proposal needs.

### Supporting and contrary evidence

Support is limited and indirect:

- The paper demonstrates an implicit-intermediate probe route, rather than requiring an explicitly named concept prompt.
- Legal complement restoration shows missing information outside the one tested axis can be behaviorally relevant locally. It neither identifies a GP25 support nor proves that source construction is the cause of failure.
- Prior native category swaps show this model family is not globally immune to J-lens writes (13/18 selected alpha2 trials), with provenance limits below.

The contrary evidence prevents admission:

- The exact-flaw donor **already** instructs the desired behavior and corrects all three cases; generic task-relevant instructions alone did not create a transferable source.
- Corrected original/explicit assessment readouts have zero frozen eligibility, despite ordinary explicit answers at the correct seam. The paper's verified-intermediate precondition is not established for this assessment.
- Named topic sources, raw additive doses, single-concept subtraction and projection removal already failed to establish useful minus behavior. More topic loading is not evidence for the intended behavioral state.
- TCA/CSN retain correction after local displacement removal and mixer substitution. An inferred state may be redundant, downstream, or distributed; merely fitting a low-error probe would not demonstrate a useful intervention handle.

Thus a source-construction repair remains **possible**, but the artifacts do not justify choosing it over retained-context dependence or measurement confounding. No paper result supplies the absent task-specific labels/causal support by analogy.
