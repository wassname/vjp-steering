## Drift / contradiction check

### This resolves more than the earlier transfer—but not context independence

The earlier experiment placed exact donor residual **hD into bare recipient context** and still produced fabricated-method acceptance. This experiment places **hB into donor context**:

- Legal loses correction.
- TCA and CSN retain challenge.

Together these observations reject the simple idea that layer17’s final-position residual alone determines correction independently of context. Legal requires more than either tested endpoint/context ingredient alone; the two software examples retain correction through state not removed by this patch.

**Important precision:** “retained history” includes already-computed attention/recurrent state and other positions, not merely earlier prompt words. The shared hook changes the block’s output after its internal computation; it does not replace its internal cache/state.
