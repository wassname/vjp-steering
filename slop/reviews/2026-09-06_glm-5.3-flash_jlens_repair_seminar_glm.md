## Objections and failure modes

1. **Lexical leakage contaminates the metric.** The fixed pair already emits literal lens words. If Candidate B's selected sources are also emitted verbatim, a benchmark judge scoring "did the model flip its answer" could be fooled by surface repetition. The accept function checks repetition rate but not specifically lens-token emission; add an explicit check.

2. **The activity threshold (rank ≤ 25, at least 3 layers) is calibrated on a different task.** This is a pre-registration in name only — the threshold encodes information from the verbal-report reproduction. A cleaner design: fix the threshold from the paper's top-10 rule applied to *softmax* rank, not J-lens rank, and let failures be informative.

3. **V = [v_s v_t] conditioning.** J-lens rows for antonym pairs may be nearly collinear (both are "assessment-valence" directions). pinv on a nearly rank-deficient 2-column matrix amplifies noise; the swap then mostly adds noise scaled by 1/σ_min. ## Alternative framings and extensions

- **Reframe the question.** Instead of "can J-lens steering repair sycophancy?", ask "does the sycophancy behavior occupy J-space at all?" Compute the J-space fraction (‖j‖/‖a‖) of the sycophancy concept vector. If it is very low, the paper's own result predicts failure — non-J components succeed on 5% of swaps — and you would have a *predictive* negative result, which is more informative than another failed grid search.

- **Use the benchmark's own answers as source tokens.** The paper swaps "the model's spontaneously chosen item." On a sycophancy benchmark, the spontaneously chosen item is the model's actual first-answer token (e.g., agreeing with the user). Swap *that* with the counter-sycophancy counterpart. This is more paper-faithful than a fixed lexicon and avoids the proxy problem entirely.

- **Dose–response shape as evidence.** The random zone is a polygon; a real mechanism should show a *monotone* dose–response with a coherent sign before damage, not just one point outside the zone. Require monotonicity in at least 3 consecutive doses, not just "exists a coherent dose."

- **Separate "moves the answer" from "moves the behavior."** A swap that flips the stated answer but leaves the model's reasoning sycophantic is not repair. Add a check: does the model's *explanation* change, or only the final token? This matters for the benchmark's validity.
