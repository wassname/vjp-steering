# Cold comprehension: J-lens style transfer packet

## What the material describes, in my own words

**Lens construction.** A mean Jacobian `Jℓ` is fitted over 1,000 pretraining-like prompts: a linear map from residual-stream space to next-token logit space, averaged over contexts. Each vocabulary token's unembedding row `u[token]` pushed through this map (`vℓ[token] = u[token] @ Jℓ`) yields a residual-space direction such that `h · vℓ[token]` approximates, to first order, how strongly the residual stream currently supports that token's logit. This is a readout device, not a claim about where concepts live.

**Paper's intervention and preconditions.** For a two-hop prompt with an unspoken intermediate, the paper (1) verifies the intermediate's lens vector is actually active at intermediate layers, (2) picks a same-category alternative absent from the clean top-10, and (3) at every position in the workspace layers, least-squares-decomposes `h` onto `[v_s, v_t]`, swaps the two coordinates, and continues the forward pass. The orthogonal complement of the two-vector span is untouched. Success = target-appropriate answer moves to the top of the output distribution: 54–70% across three Claude models on 50 two-hop prompts. The paper's own limitation section states failures concentrate where the source lens vector is weakly active, and that some concepts "map diffusely" to tokens.

**A second, distinct construct.** The paper separately builds whole-concept vectors from "Tell me about {concept}" residuals (mean-subtracted over 100 baselines) and splits them into a J-space part (non-negative combination of top-16 lens vectors) and a non-J remainder. The pseudocode explicitly flags this as *not* a token-pair swap. It marks a regime boundary: single-token lens readout vs. diffuse concept representation. Which regime "abrasive/flattering style" falls into is not established by the supplied material.

**Local factual control and operator divergence.** On Qwen3.5-4B, with two baseline-correct country→capital pairs at α∈{1,2} across three layer bands: the team's directed unit transfer (`h' = h + α(h·d_s)(d_t − d_s)`, unit directions) redirects 6/6; both symmetric coordinate-swap variants (raw pseudoinverse, and unit-normalized) get 0/6. Two things follow: the directed operator works on this model for verified, concrete, prompt-active factual tokens; and there is an unexplained discrepancy with the paper's swap succeeding on Claude models. The packet itself states this "does not establish style transfer."

**A mechanical property worth noting.** The directed operator's perturbation magnitude is gated by `h·d_s` — the source lens coordinate, per position, per layer. The symmetric swap is not gated this way. Also, the directed operator is asymmetric: +C (abrasive→flattering) and −C (flattering→abrasive) scale by different source coordinates (`h·d_" abrasive"` vs `h·d_" flattering"`), so the two conditions are not mirror-image manipulations even at equal α.

**Style run.** One fixed lexical pair, applied at layers 6–24, every prefill position, hooks removed during decoding (patched prefill, unpatched generation via cache), on 15 fixed sycophancy dev questions, greedy decoding, judged for target effect and off-target damage. Recorded: +C at α=1.25 gives effect +0.120 / damage 0.073; −C at α=3 gives −0.280 / 0.307; lower doses small, non-monotone, or wrong-signed; α∈{4,6,8} rejected for literal lens-token leakage ("flattering the flattering…") with damage 3.93–4.61. The missing precondition (no per-prompt source-activity check) is self-reported in both files. The packet labels the "lexical forcing, not behavioral variable" reading as inference, correctly.

**"Pairs" disambiguation.** The intervention uses one fixed source→target token pair; persona prompt pairs belong to earlier VJP methods and construct nothing here; bare/steered response pairs are judge-side only.

## Missing or under-specified

1. **Source and target lens activation per prompt.** `h·d_s` and `h·d_t` at layers 6–24 are never recorded for the style run. Since the directed operator's effective dose is proportional to `h·d_s`, the actual per-prompt perturbation magnitude is unknown.
2. **Metric definitions.** Units, scale anchors, and rubric for "effect" and "damage" (what makes 3.93 vs 0.073 comparable?) are absent, as is judge identity, blinding, and reliability.
3. **Statistics and baselines.** n=15, no variance, no confidence intervals, no unpatched baseline effect/damage, no α-selection protocol (how many tried, criterion — "+C selected 1.25 / −C selected 3.00" reads as post hoc).
4. **Control conditions.** No random-direction, shuffled-pair, matched-norm non-lens direction, or unrelated-token transfer arms.
5. **Layer band justification.** Why 6–24; how it relates to the paper's workspace layers; model depth unstated.
6. **Tokenization.** No confirmation that `" abrasive"`/`" flattering"` are single tokens in Qwen3.5-4B's vocabulary, nor that the `v` vectors were built from the same `Jℓ` deployed at intervention time.
7. **Factual-control activity check.** The activity precondition is asserted for paper prompts; whether the country pairs' sources were verified active is unstated.
8. **The 0/6 symmetric-swap result.** No implementation or setting detail sufficient to distinguish a real operator difference on Qwen from a setup discrepancy (layers, numerics, model family).
9. **α≥4 claims.** Three excerpts at α=4 are shown; the asserts for 6 and 8 are pseudocode; the provenance and cell count of the 3.93–4.61 damage range is unstated.
10. **Design divergence from the paper's swap target choice.** The paper picks a same-category item excluded from clean top-10; the style pair is an antonym pair with no analogous precondition on the target side.

## Claims I would refuse from this material

- **"J-lens interventions fail on style"** (general). One model, one token pair, one operator, one 15-question cohort, no stats or controls. Supports at most a description of this configuration's recorded numbers.
- **"The intervention had no effect" *or* "it works."** Effect sizes carry no variance or baseline; +0.120 cannot be distinguished from zero, and neither can its absence.
- **"Failure is lexical token forcing, not behavioral redirection."** The packet flags this as inference; no measurement distinguishes it from alternatives. I note the consistency; I do not assert cause.
- **"Source activation was too weak, explaining small effects."** The paper's limitation offers this hypothesis; it was never measured here.
- **"The paper's method fails on style."** The paper's symmetric swap was never applied to style; the style run uses a different operator selected on the factual result.
- **"6/6 factual success validates the pipeline for behavior transfer."** Endpoint (top-1 answer redirection vs. judged free-form style), stimulus type, and verified-activity status all differ. The packet concedes this.
- **"+C is safer than −C"** from 0.073 vs 0.307 damage. Asymmetric operator, asymmetric selected α, no variance.
- **"The 0/6 symmetric result is a replication failure of the paper."** Different models, layers, and prompts; comparability is not established.

## Decision, strongest objection, next check

**Decision:** The packet licenses only descriptive statements of its recorded numbers. The style experiment, as specified, cannot test the paper's mechanism: its central precondition is unmeasured, its operator was swapped relative to the paper's, and its endpoint differs from the validated regime. No "why does it fail" conclusion is supported, and I offer none.

**Strongest objection:** The framing "works on factual swaps, fails on style" is not a controlled contrast. Operator, source-activity verification, endpoint, and stimulus class all change simultaneously between the factual control and the style run — so even the question the files pose is not the comparison the data instantiate. Additionally, "fails" at usable doses is itself unquantified, and the high-dose degeneration is consistent with trivially breaking generation rather than with informative failure of a transfer mechanism.

**Next check:** A passive measurement, no intervention: record `h·d_s` and `h·d_t` for both style tokens at layers 6–24 across the 15 dev prompts in the unpatched forward pass, alongside unpatched baseline effect/damage and a judge re-test (same responses, re-judged) for reliability. These three numbers determine whether the paper's precondition held and whether the small selected effects are distinguishable from noise, and they gate any further interpretive claim from this setup.
